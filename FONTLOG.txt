This file provides detailed information on the Marjorie font software. This
information should be distributed along with the Marjorie fonts and any
derivative works.


Basic Font Information

Marjorie is a geometric display typeface with Art Deco bones — high-waisted
proportions, classical Roman skeletons, and clean monoline strokes. It was
drawn for headlines, posters, logotypes, and packaging where a single
confident voice carries the layout. One weight (Regular), 460 glyphs,
covering the basic Latin alphabet, figures, punctuation, and accented
Latin characters.

Originally designed in 2016. Released as open source under the SIL Open
Font License in 2026.
